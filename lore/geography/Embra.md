Embra is the smaller, warm amber-orange companion star of [[Aurel]], orbited together with Aurel by [[Sera]].

Locked in a tight dance with Aurel, Embra periodically passes in front of and behind its brighter partner, causing brief, regular dips in daylight across Sera. Like Aurel, Embra is a dense physical knot in the [[Twill]], and its amber light is ordinary physics rather than magic.
