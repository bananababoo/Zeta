The Nap is the misty transitive plane formed by the loose surface fibers of the [[Twill]] brushing directly against physical space and the elemental realms.

Travelers who step into its shallow border see the ordinary world through pale fog, muted in color and sound, while remaining unseen by those outside it. Deeper within the Nap, the physical world fades into drifting curtains of mist that carry travelers toward the elemental realms of [[Vayuun]], [[Tellur]], [[Suryak]], and [[Apsun]].

Pure planar beings sometimes condense out of the Nap's drifting mists, lingering as faint shapes before dissolving again when the surrounding Twill shifts.
