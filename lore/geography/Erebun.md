Erebun is an outer plane of pure [[Umbrifory]], formed from apathy, despair, and cold malice without ambition or passion.

Its 3 gray, treeless glooms lie beneath a colorless ash sky where stagnant rivers wind through muddy flats and ruined cairns. The air itself saps warmth, color, and resolve, tempting travelers to sit down in the gray dust and stop caring about anything at all.

Hollow planar beings condense out of Erebun's stagnant Umbrifory, drifting through its mists until the [[Twill]] beneath them thins or shifts.
