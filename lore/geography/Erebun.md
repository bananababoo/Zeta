Erebun is an outer plane of pure [[Umbrifory]], formed from apathy, despair, and cold malice without ambition or passion.

It lies as a windless basin of dry dust, cracked clay, and slow black reeds under a pale, unmoving haze. The still air saps warmth, color, and resolve, tempting travelers to sit down in the gray dust and stop caring about anything at all.

Hollow planar beings condense out of Erebun's stagnant Umbrifory, drifting through its reeds until the [[Twill]] beneath them thins or shifts.
