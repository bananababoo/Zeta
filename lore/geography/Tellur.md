Tellur is the elemental plane of earth, an unbroken expanse of crushing bedrock, ore seams, and slow-grinding stone left from [[Kairo]]'s earliest unfinished work.

Hollowed out within its solid mass lie vaulted caverns lit by luminous crystal veins, subterranean rivers of silt, and buried cities carved into fault lines. The stone itself shifts with glacial patience, sealing old tunnels and opening new chambers over centuries.

Pure stone and crystal beings occasionally coalesce where pressure and the [[Twill]] concentrate in Tellur, enduring only until shifting strata and currents unmake them.
