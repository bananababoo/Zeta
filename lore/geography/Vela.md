Vela is the single moon orbiting [[Sera]], reflecting the mixed light of [[Aurel]] and [[Embra]] across the night sky.

Vela's steady orbit pulls ocean tides along Sera's coastlines and stirs gentle, rhythmic tides in the [[Twill]], causing local [[Lustre]] to swell slightly at full crest and ebb at the dark of the moon. Cultures across Sera commonly mark months by Vela's phases.
