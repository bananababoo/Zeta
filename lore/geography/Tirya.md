Tirya is an overgrown, twilight-bright echo plane that mirrors tight-knot worlds like [[Sera]] where wild [[Lustre]] pools thick in the [[Twill]].

Its landscapes exaggerate the physical world's features: ordinary forests rise into towering canopies of luminous moss, quiet brooks become singing rapids, and seasons shift with the moods of the beings who dwell there. Time runs unevenly in Tirya, so a night spent in a clearing can pass as days or mere heartbeats back on Sera.

The plane's native spirits and courts form easily out of dense wild Lustre, but like all pure planar beings, their forms and loyalties shift or fade whenever the surrounding Twill changes.
