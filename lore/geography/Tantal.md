Tantal is an outer plane of [[Umbrifory]] shaped by spite, treachery, and the desire to cage or punish others.

It is arranged in 6 nested chains of drifting prison orbs—fetid swamps, acid sands, jagged crags, and frozen wastes—suspended in red gloom. Escape paths are always visible yet deliberately treacherous, designed to turn prisoners against one another.

Bitter planar beings form out of Tantal's tangled Umbrifory as jailers and saboteurs, lasting only until the spiteful currents in the [[Twill]] shift and dissolve them.
