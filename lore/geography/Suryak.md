Suryak is the elemental plane of fire, a blazing realm of ember seas, basalt crusts, and living flame left from [[Kairo]]'s earliest unfinished work.

Its cooler borderlands form cracked plains of obsidian and ash where brass-walled fortresses and forge-cities cling to stability, while its heart burns as an ocean of white-hot plasma and roaring solar plumes. Heat and light rise from the ground itself rather than any sun.

Flame-born planar beings rise from Suryak's fiercest eruptions and cinder-storms, burning bright and fiercely until the underlying heat and [[Twill]] shift and quench them.
