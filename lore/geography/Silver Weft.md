The Silver Weft is the deep, timeless transitive plane of the [[Twill]] that connects physical worlds, distant galaxies, and the outer planes.

It appears as an endless silvery sea threaded with slow-moving currents of thought, memory, and stray intention. Distance answers to focus rather than footsteps, and bodies neither age nor hunger while adrift in its currents, though both return when a traveler steps back into another realm.

Drifting islands of petrified memory and abandoned shrines float along its currents, beside shifting pools that open into [[Meridian]] and the 16 outer planes.
