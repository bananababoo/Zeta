Ululon is an outer plane of restless change leaning toward [[Umbrifory]], shaped by paranoia, isolation, and unhinged fury.

It is a boundless mass of dark stone hollowed by twisting tunnels and vaulted chasms through which freezing gales howl without pause. The wind extinguishes open flames, carries distorted whispers for miles, and scatters clear thought for anyone who cannot find deep shelter.

Frantic planar beings condense out of Ululon's screaming drafts and frayed [[Twill]], howling through the caverns until the wind and weave shift and scatter them.
