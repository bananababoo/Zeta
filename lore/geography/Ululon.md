Ululon is an outer plane of restless change leaning toward [[Umbrifory]], shaped by paranoia, isolation, and unhinged fury.

It is a maze of wind-carved badlands, hollow basalt canyons, and echoing stone arches where freezing gales howl without pause. The wind extinguishes open flames, carries distorted whispers for miles, and scatters clear thought for anyone caught outside shelter.

Frantic planar beings condense out of Ululon's screaming drafts and frayed [[Twill]], howling through the canyons until the wind and weave shift and scatter them.
