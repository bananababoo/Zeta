Skaldir is an outer plane of free [[Luminifory]] and restless change, shaped by heroic daring, rivalry, and songs of courage.

Huge mountainous earthbergs drift slowly through a glowing sky above rivers of molten stone, their ridges dotted with timbered mead-halls and wind-scoured passes. Those who fall in open contest on Skaldir's upper slopes rise again at the next dawn, healed and ready to test themselves anew.

Battle-born planar spirits coalesce out of Skaldir's surging [[Twill]], riding its storms for a span before dissolving when the currents of daring shift elsewhere.
