Skaldir is an outer plane of free [[Luminifory]] and restless change, shaped by heroic daring, rivalry, and songs of courage.

It is a vast subarctic expanse of glacier-carved fjords, glowing auroras, and storm-swept highland passes dotted with timbered halls and stone cairns. Explorers and oath-keepers sail out across ice-lit seas toward ever-new horizons, testing themselves against wild weather and worthy rivals.

Storm-born planar spirits coalesce out of Skaldir's surging [[Twill]], riding its winds for a span before dissolving when the currents of daring shift elsewhere.
