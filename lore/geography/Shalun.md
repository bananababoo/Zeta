Shalun is a muted, ash-pale echo plane that mirrors tight-knot worlds like [[Sera]] where the [[Twill]] lies thin, cold, and leached of warmth.

Every mountain, road, and settlement on Sera casts a gaunt reflection in Shalun—ruined, weathered, or warped into hollow stone under a sunless gray sky. Memories and lingering grief cling to its places like cold dew, and travelers who remain too long feel their urgency and color slowly drain away.

Shadowy planar beings form where old sorrow or forgotten [[Umbrifory]] settles in Shalun, persisting only until the lingering weave beneath them thins out or shifts.
