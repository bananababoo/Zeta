Amunor is a crescent-shaped continent at the center of [[Sera]]'s oceans, curving around a sheltered central bay.

Sun-baked arid plains dominate Amunor's southern and interior reach, cut by winding fertile river corridors that empty into its northern and western coasts. Its central position places it within sailing distance of [[Byblon]], [[Hhorun]], [[Luwara]], and [[Matara]].
