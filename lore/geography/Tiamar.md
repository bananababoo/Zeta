Tiamar is the churning elemental rim where the outer edges of [[Vayuun]], [[Tellur]], [[Suryak]], and [[Apsun]] collide and unmake one another.

Nothing holds stable form for long in Tiamar: boiling seas crash into flying slabs of molten basalt, ash-storms freeze into hail mid-air, and whole landscapes tear apart in moments. It is the rawest remnant of [[Kairo]]'s earliest unfinished knots in the [[Twill]].

Tempestuous elemental beings flare into existence where opposing currents clash in Tiamar, lasting only as long as the storm that birthed them.
