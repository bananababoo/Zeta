Auster is a southern polar continent on [[Sera]], locked inside cold circumpolar ocean currents.

Glaciers and wind-scoured ice sheets blanket most of Auster's interior, broken only by rocky coastal headlands, tundra rims, and scattered geothermal vents beneath the ice.
