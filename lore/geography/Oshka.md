Oshka is a vast southern continent on [[Sera]] stretching from warm mid-latitudes down to the cold sub-polar ocean.

High snow-capped mountains rise along Oshka's northwestern rim, casting cool windswept steppes and river valleys across its center before its southern coast fractures into deep fjords, cold bays, and offshore island chains. North of its western peaks, a shallow shelf sea sits ringed by volcanic islands.
