Vayuun is the boundless elemental plane of air, left from [[Kairo]]'s earliest unfinished work as an endless sky without ground or ceiling.

Drifting islands of wind-carved stone, cloud-banks, and frozen rain hang suspended in vast trade winds that sweep across the plane for ages. Storms gather into roaring corridors where lightning leaps between floating crags, while calmer layers hold clear air and drifting palaces built by travelers.

Elemental air spirits condense out of Vayuun's strongest gales and pressure fronts, holding shape for a time before dispersing back into the wind as currents change.
