Narak is an outer plane of ordered [[Umbrifory]], formed from cold tyranny, exploitation, and cruelly enforced hierarchies.

It is a sprawling labyrinth of black-basalt courts, frozen archives, and furnace-lit mints governed by iron ledgers, binding oaths, and unforgiving chains of command. Every hall and contract in Narak is built to trap the weak in endless debt and obedience.

Calculating planar beings condense out of Narak's dense Umbrifory to enforce its bargains, persisting only while the tyrannical currents in the [[Twill]] that shaped them hold steady.
