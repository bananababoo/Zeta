Aurel is the larger, bright yellow-white star of the binary pair orbited by [[Sera]], formed as 1 of the tightest knots in the [[Twill]].

Aurel and its smaller companion [[Embra]] orbit closely around a shared center, rising and setting together in Sera's sky and casting subtle double shadows near dawn and dusk. Aurel provides most of Sera's daylight and warmth; its sunlight is ordinary physical light, separate from [[Luminifory]] and [[Umbrifory]].
