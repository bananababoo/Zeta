Byblon is a west-central continent on [[Sera]] separated from [[Hhorun]] by a narrow diagonal strait.

A deep enclosed inland sea sits at the heart of Byblon, ringed by coastal hills, river valleys, and broad sunlit plains. To the south, a fractured archipelago and sheltered gulfs open toward the cold southern sea and [[Oshka]].
