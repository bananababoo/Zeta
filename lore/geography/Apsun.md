Apsun is the elemental plane of water, an endless ocean without shore, surface, or seabed left from [[Kairo]]'s earliest unfinished work.

Warm sunlit currents drift through its upper reaches around tangled kelp forests and drifting coral reefs, while its deep abysses grow dark, frigid, and heavy under immense pressure. Pockets of breathable air trapped in glass-like shells or floating icebergs serve as havens for visitors from physical worlds.

Water-born planar beings form along Apsun's colliding tides and thermal vents, shifting form with the currents until they dissolve back into the sea.
