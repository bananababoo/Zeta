Luwara is a long volcanic island continent on [[Sera]], running north to south along a deep oceanic subduction trench between [[Hhorun]], [[Amunor]], and [[Matara]].

A steep volcanic mountain spine runs down Luwara's length, feeding short coastal rivers and sheltering lush eastern slopes while smaller volcanic islands and coral shoals trail off its southern tip.
