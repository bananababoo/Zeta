Matara is the largest continent on [[Sera]], a twin-lobed equatorial landmass covering roughly a quarter of the planet's land.

A deep tectonic rift lake and coastal fjord divide Matara's western and eastern halves, bordered by snow-capped alpine peaks to the north and south. Dense monsoon river networks drain its humid interior plains into lush coastal deltas, while rain shadows behind its higher ridges carve drier upland basins. South and east of its coast lie volcanic islands above an active ocean trench.
