Kavar is an outer plane of [[Umbrifory]] shaped by ruthless greed, betrayal, and self-interest at any cost.

It is a glittering, treacherous waste of white salt-crust, glass-edged sinkholes, false oases, and collapsing bridges built over sunken treasure-pits. Every shelter and trade post hides a snare, and travelers learn quickly that generosity here always carries a blade behind it.

Cunning planar beings form out of Kavar's bitter Umbrifory, trading favors and ambushes until shifting currents in the [[Twill]] change or dissolve them.
