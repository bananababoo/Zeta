Eunomia is an outer plane of harmony and civic order leaning toward [[Luminifory]], shaped by collective discipline and shared duty.

Its orchards grow in exact rows, its rivers flow along dressed stone channels, and even its clouds arrive on a predictable schedule so that crops never wither or flood. Every hill and meadow feels tended for the common good, leaving little room for surprise or wildness.

Orderly planar beings form from Eunomia's steady [[Twill]] to keep its paths and irrigation true, changing or fading whenever the plane's balance shifts.
