Eunomia is an outer plane of civic harmony leaning toward [[Luminifory]], shaped by collective discipline, fairness, and shared duty.

Walled stone garden-cities stand linked by clear aqueducts, shaded courtyards, and well-kept granaries where drought and want are kept at bay by steady stewardship. Every road and waterway is tended for the common good, leaving little room for surprise or wildness.

Orderly planar beings form from Eunomia's steady [[Twill]] to keep its paths and waterways true, changing or fading whenever the plane's balance shifts.
