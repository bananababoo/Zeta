Maelun is an outer plane of pure change, balanced between [[Luminifory]] and [[Umbrifory]] and formed from restless curiosity, impulse, and refusal to stay still.

It is a roiling sea of unformed stone, cloud, flame, and water with no fixed ground or sky. Solid islands of earth, monasteries, and towns exist here only while focused minds hold their shape against the surrounding flux; when attention lapses, the matter unravels back into the swirl.

Protean planar beings emerge from Maelun's shifting [[Twill]], changing form constantly before dissolving back into the tide.
