Tarnok is an outer plane of rigid order leaning toward [[Umbrifory]], shaped by blind obedience, conquest, and endless war.

Vast cubes of dark iron—some the size of continents—drift through a dim void, scraping and colliding with thunderous impacts that rattle every fortress riveted to their faces. Marching columns, rusted battlefields, and supply depots cover their flat planes, fighting campaigns whose original causes were forgotten ages ago.

Armored planar beings condense out of Tarnok's grim [[Twill]], fighting until the currents of war and obedience shift and unmake them.
