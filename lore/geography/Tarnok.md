Tarnok is an outer plane of rigid order leaning toward [[Umbrifory]], shaped by blind obedience, conquest, and endless war.

It stretches out as a bleak, fog-bound steppe scarred by endless stone ramparts, dry trenches, and ruined watchtowers. Marching columns and supply trains grind along its border fortifications, fighting campaigns whose original causes were forgotten ages ago.

Armored planar beings condense out of Tarnok's grim [[Twill]], fighting until the currents of war and obedience shift and unmake them.
