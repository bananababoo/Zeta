Meridian is the central outer plane where [[Luminifory]] and [[Umbrifory]], order and change, meet in quiet balance.

It stretches out as a wide, rolling plain around a single towering spire at its heart, where magic grows calmer and quieter the closer one approaches the center. Along Meridian's outer rim stand 16 border settlements, each leaning in architecture, weather, and temper toward the neighboring outer plane that its archway opens onto.

Because no single lean dominates Meridian, pure planar beings rarely condense here on their own, and travelers from every realm meet on neutral ground.
