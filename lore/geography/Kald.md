Kald is a northern sub-arctic continent on [[Sera]], lying northeast of [[Luwara]] and north of [[Matara]].

Glacial lakes, taiga forests, and jagged cold-water bays cover Kald's rolling shield terrain, with snowfields and tundra along its northern shoreline.
