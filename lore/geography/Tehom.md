Tehom is an outer plane of free [[Umbrifory]], formed from senseless cruelty, wrath, and wanton destruction.

It falls away through countless mutating layers of ruin—blistered plains, boiling seas, bone-choked jungles, and collapsing chasms—with no fixed count or lasting borders. Whole landscapes warp and devour one another as waves of destructive intent wash through the [[Twill]].

Feral planar beings tear free from Tehom's turbulent Umbrifory, raging and devouring until the shifting weave alters or unmakes them.
