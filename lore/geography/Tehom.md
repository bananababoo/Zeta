Tehom is an outer plane of free [[Umbrifory]], formed from senseless cruelty, wrath, and wanton destruction.

It is a lightless, churning ocean of pitch-black brine, jagged bone-reefs, and collapsing sea-trenches where waves of destructive intent constantly warp and devour the landscape itself. Nothing built here endures for long before the deep swallows it.

Feral planar beings tear free from Tehom's turbulent Umbrifory, raging and devouring until the shifting weave alters or unmakes them.
