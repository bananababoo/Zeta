Othra is an outer plane of [[Luminifory]] shaped by honest craft, hospitality, and shared stewardship.

It stretches across sheltered river valleys, terraced orchards, cedar workshops, and warm stone kilns folded between misty green ridges. Tools hold their edge, hearths burn clean, and every valley reflects the care of people who build things meant to last for neighbors they may never meet.

Planar spirits of craft and shelter occasionally form from Othra's steady Luminifory, tending groves and forges until the surrounding [[Twill]] shifts and calls them back into the weave.
