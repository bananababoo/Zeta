Othra is an outer plane of [[Luminifory]] shaped by honest craft, shared labor, and mutual stewardship.

It consists of 2 facing worlds suspended overhead like a vaulted sky, one a gentle pastoral expanse of terraced farms and workshops and the other a rugged wilderness of timber, ore, and mountain streams. Travelers can look straight up and see the lights of the opposite hemisphere shining through thin drifting clouds.

Planar spirits of craft and shelter occasionally form from Othra's steady Luminifory, tending groves and forges until the surrounding [[Twill]] shifts and calls them back into the weave.
