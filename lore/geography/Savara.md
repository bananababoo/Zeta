Savara is an outer plane of wild [[Luminifory]], formed from reverence for living creatures and the untamed natural world.

It is layered into 3 vast skies of unbroken nature: an upper realm of perpetual bright noon, a middle expanse of long golden twilight, and a lower canopy of starlit night. Great herds, ancient predators, and sentient beasts roam its forests, savannas, and rivers without cities or walls.

Animal-shaped planar spirits form out of Savara's living Luminifory, roaming its trails for a span before dissolving or changing as the [[Twill]] shifts.
