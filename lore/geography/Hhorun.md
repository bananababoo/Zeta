Hhorun is the second-largest continent on [[Sera]], spanning the northern temperate and boreal latitudes.

Broad interior steppes and wooded plains stretch across Hhorun, fed by long glacial watersheds that branch northward to the polar sea and southward toward the warmer straits separating Hhorun from [[Byblon]]. Cold alpine ridges and high passes frame its northern and western margins.
