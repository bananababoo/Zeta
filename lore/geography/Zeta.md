Zeta is the universe, made up of mostly ordinary space with rare strange exceptions.

Zeta holds billions of galaxies, which follow large currents in the [[Twill]], and stars, which are its tightest knots. A few anomaly worlds are left over from the early experiments of [[Kairo]].

Worlds form in 3 ways:

- **Early experiments:** The oldest worlds, made by Kairo out of curiosity, are often strange, lopsided, unfinished, or abandoned.
- **Knots in the Twill:** Most worlds form where threads of the Twill tangle and pull tight. Tight knots make dense, stable worlds, and loose knots make strange, shifting ones.
- **Worlds born from worlds:** Rarely, a world forms when a world's people build up enough intention over ages.

A world's origin shapes its land and environment, never its people's morality. No one is born good or evil.

Beyond ordinary physical space, Zeta holds layered planes woven from the [[Twill]]:

- **Transitive planes:** [[Nap|The Nap]] brushes against physical worlds and elemental space as a misty surface weave, while [[Silver Weft|The Silver Weft]] carries deep currents between worlds and outer realms.
- **Echo planes:** [[Tirya]] and [[Shalun]] mirror tight-knot worlds like [[Sera]], one overflowing with wild [[Lustre]] and the other leached and muted.
- **Inner planes:** [[Vayuun]], [[Tellur]], [[Suryak]], [[Apsun]], and the churning rim of [[Tiamar]] are raw elemental realms left from [[Kairo]]'s earliest unfinished work and primordial knots.
- **Outer planes:** Born over ages from the [[Gathering of the Outer Planes]], 16 realms ring the central hub of [[Meridian]]: [[Solmira]], [[Othra]], [[Kalyra]], [[Savara]], [[Thyrra]], [[Skaldir]], [[Eunomia]], [[Taktos]], [[Maelun]], [[Tarnok]], [[Narak]], [[Solfar]], [[Erebun]], [[Tantal]], [[Tehom]], and [[Ululon]].

Where the Twill concentrates deeply in a plane, pure planar beings can form directly out of its substance. Such beings are temporary: as intentions and the Twill shift around them, they change in kind or dissolve back into the cloth.
