Solmira is an outer plane of ordered [[Luminifory]], rising as a single terraced mountain through 7 clear heavens out of a tranquil silver sea.

Born from centuries of disciplined mercy, kept vows, and steadfast duty, its lower slopes hold terraced white-stone towns and terraced orchards that give way to sunlit peaks and quiet sanctuaries above the clouds. Every slope rewards patience and honest sacrifice rather than force.

Radiant planar beings form where Solmira's Luminifory gathers thickest, serving as guardians and guides until shifting currents in the [[Twill]] reshape or disperse them.
