Solmira is an outer plane of ordered [[Luminifory]], shaped by centuries of disciplined mercy, kept vows, and steadfast duty.

It unfolds as a wide highland of sunlit limestone terraces, clear reed-waters, and open-air colonnades where honest promises echo faintly in the air like distant song. Travelers find shelter easily in its hill-sanctuaries, and the land itself rewards patience and restraint over force.

Radiant planar beings form where Solmira's Luminifory gathers thickest, serving as guides and guardians until shifting currents in the [[Twill]] reshape or disperse them.
