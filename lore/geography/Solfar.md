Solfar is an outer plane of [[Umbrifory]] shaped by ruthless greed, betrayal, and self-interest at any cost.

It consists of 4 steep volcanic mountains hanging in open dark, their sulfurous slopes so pitched that almost no flat ground exists anywhere. Rockslides, acid vents, and rivers of cooling slag force every shelter to cling precariously to narrow ledges while neighbors eye each other's footing.

Cunning planar beings form out of Solfar's acrid Umbrifory, trading favors and ambushes until shifting currents in the [[Twill]] change or dissolve them.
