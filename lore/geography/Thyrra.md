Thyrra is an outer plane of free [[Luminifory]], shaped by fierce joy, art, generosity, and defiance of tyranny.

Its landscapes are bold and unrestrained: towering ancient groves whose leaves sing in the wind, wine-dark coasts battered by warm surf, and sunlit deserts dotted with singing stones. Weather and mood move quickly here, turning from laughter to sudden summer storms and back to clear sky.

Fiery, passionate planar beings condense from Thyrra's bright [[Lustre]], celebrating and aiding the bold until shifting intentions in the [[Twill]] transform or scatter them.
