Taktos is an outer plane of pure order, balanced between [[Luminifory]] and [[Umbrifory]] and shaped by devotion to law, symmetry, and exact measure.

It consists of interlocking continent-sized wheels of bronze and dressed stone turning silently through open space, their teeth meshing without friction or error. Cities, archives, and courts sit across the flat faces of the wheels, running on strict timetables without regard for mercy or malice.

Geometric planar beings condense out of Taktos's rigid [[Twill]] to maintain its rhythms, dissolving back into the weave if the patterns that formed them shift.
