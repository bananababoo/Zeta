Luminifory is the light-bearing aspect of the [[Twill]], and it forms around good acts and good intentions.

The name comes from the Latin luminiferous, meaning light-bearing. Luminifory is not physical light.

Luminifory burns brightest when someone stands against strong [[Umbrifory]].
