The Twill is an invisible woven fabric that runs through all of space and has 2 opposing aspects, [[Luminifory]] and [[Umbrifory]].

The universe distinguishes right from wrong, and the Twill is how it does so. Luminifory forms around good acts and good intentions, and Umbrifory forms around wrong acts and wrong intentions. The two are not separate things but 2 sides of 1 cloth, the way a woven cloth has a face and a back.

Intent sets the strength of what forms, and the act sets its direction. Only sincere acts and intentions affect the Twill, and staged, faked, or performative acts produce nothing.

The Twill is not physical light or darkness. Sunlight and shadow are ordinary physics, and the Twill is invisible to ordinary sight.

Ordinary people cannot perceive the Twill or anyone's true intentions. Magicians, meaning all spellcasters, can sense it, but rarely with certainty: a magician can often tell that strong energy is present without telling which aspect it is.

The Twill lingers on places and objects touched by an act and fades over time unless something preserves it, such as a consecrated temple, cursed ground, a relic, or an in-world record.

How strong magic is in a place depends on how much of the 2 aspects the Twill holds there, described in the [[Lustre]]. What counts as good or wrong is described in the [[Measure of Right]].
