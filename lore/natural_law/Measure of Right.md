The Measure of Right is the standard the [[Twill]] uses to judge every sincere act: an act is good if it aims to reduce suffering or increase the wellbeing of others, and wrong if it causes suffering without a good reason.

Every being that can suffer counts, including animals and monsters, and beings with richer minds count for more.

Causing suffering has a good reason only when it is the least harm needed to prevent greater suffering, such as defending oneself, protecting others, or hunting to eat, or when the one who suffers freely consents, such as to surgery or a sparring match. Revenge, cruelty, and harm done for profit or sport never have a good reason.

Justified harm still leaves a mark. Killing a raider to save a village creates [[Luminifory]] for the protection and weak [[Umbrifory]] for the harm, so someone who fights only to protect stays mostly Luminifory but is never spotless.
