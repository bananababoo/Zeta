The Lustre is the strength of magic in a place, set by how much [[Luminifory]] and [[Umbrifory]] the [[Twill]] holds there, added together.

The name comes from the sheen on woven cloth, which is brighter where the cloth is thick. Where little has been done or intended, the Twill is thin and magic is weak. Where strong acts of either kind have gathered, it is thick and magic is strong.

The larger aspect sets the Lustre's lean, which changes how magic behaves in that place but not who can cast there.

Where both aspects are strong, the Lustre is bright but unstable. Magic there is powerful and hard to control, because the Twill pulls it both ways at once. Such a place is different from an empty one, even though neither leans.

The possibility of wrong choices is the price of free will. If no one could choose wrong, choosing good would mean nothing, and the Twill would stay thin and still.
