Umbrifory is the shadow-bearing aspect of the [[Twill]], and it forms around wrong acts and wrong intentions.

The name comes from the Latin umbriferous, meaning shadow-bearing. Umbrifory is not physical darkness.

Harm done with good intent creates weak Umbrifory, and harm done with bad intent creates strong Umbrifory.

Wrongdoing is bad in itself, and Umbrifory is not secretly good or necessary. Umbrifory strengthens the [[Lustre]] as much as [[Luminifory]] does, but Luminifory alone can make a place just as strong in magic.
