Kairo is a true neutral god and the first being capable of real intentions, born from the [[First Crest]].

Kairo began childlike and curious. Its first act, the [[First Choice]], was to create, and that choice made the first [[Umbrifory]]. Its later acts balance this out, so Kairo is true neutral overall.
