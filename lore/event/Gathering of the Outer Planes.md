The Gathering of the Outer Planes is the slow, ongoing formation of outer realms from the accumulated intentions of mortal worlds across [[Zeta]].

Over ages, sincere acts of [[Luminifory]] and [[Umbrifory]], alongside lasting habits of order and change, built up enough weight in the [[Twill]] to bud off entire realms beyond ordinary space. As long as beings across Zeta continue to choose and intend, these realms continue to grow, shift, and reshape.
