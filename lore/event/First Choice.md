The First Choice was [[Kairo]]'s decision to create, made out of curiosity to see goodness exist.

Kairo had 2 options. The objectively good option was to let the [[First Crest]] fade back into stillness, so that nothing would ever exist or suffer. Kairo chose to create.

Kairo sincerely believed it was doing good. Bringing suffering into existence was objectively wrong, so the First Choice created the first [[Umbrifory]].
