The Formation of Aurel and Embra was when the binary stars [[Aurel]] and [[Embra]] collapsed and ignited as 2 tight knots in the [[Twill]] within the galaxy [[Yonder]], billions of years after the [[Formation of Yonder]] once earlier generations of stars had seeded the galaxy with heavy elements.
