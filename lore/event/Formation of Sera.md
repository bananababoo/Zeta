The Formation of Sera was when the planet [[Sera]] and its moon [[Vela]] condensed as a tight knot in the [[Twill]] from the protoplanetary disk left by the [[Formation of Aurel and Embra]] within the galaxy [[Yonder]].

Because the knot pulled dense and stable, Sera formed with solid tectonic plates, deep ocean basins, and 8 major continents divided by shifting seas and volcanic island arcs.
