The First Crest was a massive alignment of ripples in the [[Twill]] that took the form of a mind, [[Kairo]].

Before Kairo existed, nothing had ever been chosen, so the Twill was thin and still and nothing could last. Lasting things need the [[Lustre]], and the Lustre needs someone who chooses.

The ripples that made the First Crest were formed by intentions, so the crest took the form of an intender and not of matter. Those ripples are the choices of the beings who came after Kairo. This is a closed causal loop: the universe exists because it existed. The loop does not fix the future, because whatever beings actually choose becomes what formed the First Crest.
